Financial Report Visual Direction

Which palette feels right for a professional real estate financial tool?

NAVY + GOLD

Navy & Gold — Classic Finance

Investment bank annual report feel. Deep navy backgrounds, warm gold accents, serif headlines, structured layouts. Traditional, authoritative, premium.

CHARCOAL + GREEN

Charcoal & Green — Modern Analytical

Bloomberg-meets-modern-finance. Charcoal canvas, green accent for positive numbers, clean sans-serif, data-dense tables. Precise, fast, analytical.

CREAM + SLATE

Warm Slate — Annual Report

High-end annual report aesthetic. Warm off-white paper ground, slate text, minimal color, thin hairline rules, generous whitespace. Trustworthy, editorial.

STEEL BLUE — Brokerage Statement

Steel Blue — Brokerage Statement

Clean brokerage statement look. Dark steel blue header bar, light canvas body, tabular numbers everywhere, minimal chrome. Numbers are the hero.
